How it works: Clarity enlarges your image using high-quality stepped resampling, then applies an unsharp mask to recover perceived crispness. This increases the pixel count, dimensions, and file size. It enhances clarity but cannot invent detail that was never captured — for that, AI super-resolution (e.g. Real-ESRGAN) is the next step.
